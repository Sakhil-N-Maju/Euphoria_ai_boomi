# Mentor Pitch — Team Euphoria

AI BHOOMI programme · 23 Oct 2026

## Short pitch (for a mentor)

We're Team Euphoria, building an AI growth partner for Indian creators under the AI BHOOMI programme, with support for educators and other talking-head video makers. Editors like Cardboard (YC W26) cut videos from instructions, but they stop at the edit and are built for English-speaking marketing teams. We close the loop. We connect to the creator's Instagram and YouTube analytics, find where viewers drop off and what top creators in their niche do differently, and automatically edit future videos to fix it. Before posting, we predict drop-off points. After posting, we read comments in Malayalam, Hindi and code-mixed language to suggest what to make next, or for educators, where students are getting confused. Every video makes the system smarter, and our anonymised benchmarks across regional creators become data no global tool has. We're starting with Indian-language talking-head creators, validating through a done-for-you service, then expanding to educators.

## What makes it different

| Compared with | What they do | What we add |

| Cardboard (YC W26) | Agentic browser editor for English-speaking growth and marketing teams, from $60 a month | Indian-language first, priced for Indian creators, and analytics feed the edit |

The loop no one else covers: **Plan** (what to shoot) → **Edit** → **Post** → **Measure** (analytics and comments) → **Learn** → back to Plan.

## Who it's for

- **Primary:** Malayalam and Manglish talking-head creators.
- **Secondary:** educators (coaching institutes, teachers) and small businesses. They use the same talking-head format and the same pipeline.

## Growth features

- **Retention-driven editing (decided):** find where viewers drop off and edit future videos to avoid those moments. YouTube's Analytics API gives the full retention curve. Instagram's API gives averages, so the first version reads a screenshot of the in-app retention graph.
- **Growth coach (decided):** compare the creator's stats with public videos of successful creators in the same niche, and explain the gap in the creator's language. When the niche is very new, learn from adjacent niches and general patterns.
- **Proposed roadmap:** pre-publish drop-off prediction, hook variants, comment mining, "what to shoot next" briefs, and anonymised creator benchmarks.

## Plan

1. MVP: Malayalam and Manglish talking-head editor (raw clip in, finished Reel out).
2. Smallest growth version: retention curve or screenshot → AI marks drop-offs → the next edit fixes them.
3. Done-for-you validation with creators, then a few educators.
4. Pricing, unit economics and startup metrics after the MVP; team, legal and funding after the company is formed.
