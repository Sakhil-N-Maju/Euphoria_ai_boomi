      The problem

      Great takes, buried in a messy timeline

      Every first take has pauses, “umm, athu, matlab” and lines you said twice. Cleaning it by hand eats the evening, and most editors were built for English.

      English-first editors stumble on Malayalam speech, code-mixed lines and Indian scripts in captions.

      Viewers leave here

      No idea where viewers leave

      You see the views, but not the moment people swipe away, or what to change in the next video.

      How Euphoria fixes it

      An editor that reads your words, then cuts the video

      Euphoria transcribes your take word by word. The AI writes an edit list that can only point at words you actually said, and FFmpeg renders the cut. Nothing is invented, and every cut can be undone.
