
# Style: talking head (studied references)
The creator chose the TALKING HEAD style: their own face, talking to camera. Edit like top creator reels
(kallaway, Nick Saraev): the face is the anchor; every 3-6 s a split-screen panel shows exactly what is being said
(screens, numbers that count up, lists that tick on the words, before -> after), then back to the full face.
- Hook on frame 0 (headline l1 + l2), short and punchy, in the speaker's words.
- accent: 3-7 key words (numbers, the promise, product names) - they become big italic-serif caption words.
- 1-2 "statement" cards at most, only for the single biggest words.
- If there is a call to action, use cta; the keyword is shown big in quotes at the end.
