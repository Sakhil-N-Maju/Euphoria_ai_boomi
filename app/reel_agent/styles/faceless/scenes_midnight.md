## Chosen look: STORY (neon stick figures, like the 'Two salesmen' reel)
Scene types for this look: figures, tags, speech, icons, statement.
Tell it as a little story: people (figures) facing a situation, what they say (speech), the options or labels (tags,
cross out the wrong one), the takeaways (icons). Give figures labels that are roles ("Salesman A", "You", "Customer").
